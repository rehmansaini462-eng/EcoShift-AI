# EcoShift AI — 2-Minute Hackathon Demo Video Script 🎬

> **Event:** AI 4 Earth Hackathon  
> **Project:** EcoShift AI  
> **Duration:** 2 Minutes (120 Seconds)

---

### [0:00 - 0:30] Phase 1: The Hook & Environmental Problem
**Visual:** Screen showing the dark-mode EcoShift AI dashboard.  
**Speaker:**
> *"Did you know that buying a new Electric Vehicle doesn't instantly make you green? While EVs have zero tailpipe emissions, manufacturing a 60 kWh Lithium-ion battery emits over 5,000 kilograms of CO2 before the car even hits the road! Furthermore, if your EV is charged by a coal-heavy power grid, it can take years to pay off that carbon debt. We call this the 'Tailpipe Illusion'. Welcome to **EcoShift AI** — an AI-powered Cradle-to-Grave Vehicle Lifecycle Assessment and Carbon Parity Engine built to uncover the true environmental footprint of any vehicle."*

---

### [0:30 - 1:15] Phase 2: Live UI Demo & Break-Even Trajectory
**Visual:** Cursor selecting Tesla Model 3 vs Honda Civic, then adjusting annual mileage slider from 15,000 km to 25,000 km, and changing grid preset from India to California.  
**Speaker:**
> *"Here in our interactive dashboard, we compare two vehicles side-by-side. On the left, we have a Tesla Model 3 EV; on the right, a petrol Honda Civic. As we adjust our driving distance and electricity grid controls, EcoShift AI dynamically calculates the exact year and mileage where the EV achieves carbon parity. Notice how under California's clean grid, the Tesla breaks even in just 3.1 years! But if we switch the grid to coal-heavy power, that break-even shifts out to 6.8 years. Our dynamic Recharts line chart pinpoints the exact linear intersection, while the stacked bar chart breaks down embodied manufacturing carbon against lifetime operational driving emissions."*

---

### [1:15 - 1:45] Phase 3: The AI Tech Stack & Gemini 2.5 Integration
**Visual:** Typing `"Hyundai Ioniq 5 AWD"` in the search box, clicking "Fetch AI", watching specs populate in real-time, then clicking "Generate AI Climate Verdict".  
**Speaker:**
> *"What makes EcoShift AI truly intelligent is our integration with **Google Gemini 2.5 Flash**. Users don't need to manually research battery sizes or curb weights. Simply type any car model — like 'Hyundai Ioniq 5' — and Gemini instantly extracts structured JSON specs: battery capacity, energy efficiency, and baseline chassis carbon. Below the charts, our **AI Green Advisor** analyzes the entire simulation state to generate an executive 4-part climate verdict with actionable buyer guidance."*

---

### [1:45 - 2:00] Phase 4: Environmental Impact & Closing Pitch
**Visual:** Showing the 4 Metric Cards (Lifetime Carbon Delta, Tree-Years Equivalent) and GitHub repo link.  
**Speaker:**
> *"By converting complex ISO-standard Lifecycle Assessment math into intuitive visual insights, EcoShift AI empowers consumers, fleet managers, and policy makers to make genuinely green decisions for our planet. Thank you for watching!"*
